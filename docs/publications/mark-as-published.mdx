# Mark as Published

**Steps**

1. Click **MARK AS PUBLISHED**.

**Result**

- The Publication Details Form status has been set to **Published**.
- The Publication Details Form and publication will be viewable in the [Publication Explorer](../portal-basics/publication-explorer).

## Delete a Publication Details Form {/* #delete-a-publication-details-form */}

There may be a situation where a **Publication Record** has been created but is no longer needed on the OSP. For example, a duplicate Publication Record may have been created, and only one should remain on the OSP.

**Steps**

1. Click **Delete Publication**.
2. Confirm action.

**Result**

- The Publication Details Form has been deleted from the OSP.

**Additional Information**

- A Publication Details Form created through the [Common Workflow Steps](../manuscript-record-forms/#common-process-steps) cannot be deleted.
- You must be the creator or have the role Chief Editor to delete a Publication Details Form.
