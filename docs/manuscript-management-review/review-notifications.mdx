# Review Notifications

The OSP will send Manuscript Management Review related email notifications when actions are taken or when you have outstanding actions to take.

- When an author has selected you for Manuscript Management Review.
- When another manager refers or reassigns you a Manuscript Management Review.
- When an author Unsubmits their manuscript from Manuscript Management Review.
- Once a week with a summary of your pending Manuscript Management Reviews.
- 2-working days before the 10-working day timeline is reached.
- Once a working-day if a Manuscript Management Review is overdue.
