<details>

<summary>
### I want to submit my manuscript as two different publication types {/* #i-want-to-submit-my-manuscript-as-two-different-publication-types */}
</summary>

To submit your manuscript as two different publication types, two separate Manuscript Record Forms must be created. Both MRFs must go through the publication process.

For example, if you wanted to submit your manuscript to a Third-Party Publisher and to a Pre-Print Server, you must create a Manuscript Record Form of type **Third-Party Publication** and another Manuscript Record Form of type **Pre-Print**. Both of these MRFs must undergo their own Manuscript Management Review. 

**Steps**

1. Create and populate MRF of first Publication type.
2. [Duplicate the Manuscript Record Form](./common-features/more-features.mdx#duplicate-a-manuscript-record-form) and select the second Publication type.
3. Reupload the Manuscript PDF to the second MRF.
4. Submit both for Manuscript Management Review.

**Result**

Two MRFs of different Publication types but with the same content have been created.

</details>
