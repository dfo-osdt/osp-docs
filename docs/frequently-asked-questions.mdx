<details>

<summary>
### I want to submit my manuscript as two different publication types {/* #i-want-to-submit-my-manuscript-as-two-different-publication-types */}
</summary>

To submit your manuscript as two different publication types, two separate Manuscript Record Forms must be created. Both MRFs must go through the publication process.

For example, if you wanted to submit your manuscript to a Third-Party Publisher and to a Preprint Server, you must create a Manuscript Record Form of type **Third-Party Publication** and another Manuscript Record Form of type **Pre-Print**. Both of these MRFs must undergo their own Manuscript Management Review. 

**Steps**

1. Create and populate MRF of first Publication type.
2. [**Duplicate the Manuscript Record Form**](./manuscript-record-forms/manuscript-record-form-features.mdx#duplicate-a-manuscript-record-form) and select the second Publication type.
3. Re-upload the Manuscript PDF to the second MRF.
4. Submit both for Manuscript Management Review.

**Result**

- Two MRFs of different Publication types but with the same content have been created.

</details>

## Common Features {/* #common-features */}

### Author and Affiliate Management {/* #author-and-affiliate-management */}
<details>

<summary>
#### What email or affiliation do I use for an author who no longer works at DFO? {/* #what-email-or-affiliation-do-i-use-for-an-author-who-no-longer-works-at-dfo */}
</summary>
- If the author was employed by DFO while they contributed to the paper, their DFO email should be used and their affiliation should be Fisheries and Oceans Canada.
- If the author was no employed by DFO, or any other institution, when they contributed to the manuscript, their personal email should be used and the Independant Researcher toggle should be set to True.
  - It is highly recommended that an ORCID be associated with the author if they are a Independant Researcher.
</details>

### Author Profile {/* #author-profile */}

<details>

<summary>
#### What is the purpose of the Independant Researcher toggle? {/* #what-is-the-purpose-of-the-independant-researcher-toggle */}
</summary>
The Independant Researcher toggle allows for more accurate lifecycle management for authors. An author is eligible to become an Independant Researcher when they are no longer representing an institution due to retirement or job change, for example.
</details>

<details>
<summary>
#### Will my affiliation associated with my publication records change if change affiliations or become an Independant Researcher? {/* #will-my-affiliation-associated-with-my-publication-records-change-if-change-affiliations-or-become-an-independant-researcher */}
</summary>
No, changing affiliations will not overwrite your author reference on existing publication records. Only records created after your affiliation change will show your new affiliation.
</details>
